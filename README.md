# CFO Financial Performance & Profitability Analysis

## NovaMart Consumer Products Pvt. Ltd.

A financial performance and profitability analysis project using **Excel, SQL, and Power BI** to evaluate revenue, costs, profitability, regional performance, product performance, and budget variances.

**Analysis Period:** April 2025 – March 2026

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The key management question for this project is:

> **How is revenue performing, and is the company becoming more profitable?**

The analysis focuses on revenue, COGS, gross profit, operating expenses, EBITDA, net profit, regional performance, category and product profitability, monthly trends, and budget vs actual performance.

## 🔎 Key Business Insights

### 1. Maharashtra has the lowest profitability

Maharashtra generated approximately **₹4.01M in revenue** and recorded the lowest regional Net Profit Margin at **14.40%**.

This indicates a need to review pricing, COGS, operating expenses, and product mix in the region.

### 2. Electronics Accessories is a major profitability concern in Maharashtra

Electronics Accessories recorded a Net Profit Margin of only **9.04%** in Maharashtra.

All four products in the category had margins below 10%, indicating a broader category-level profitability issue rather than a problem with a single product.

### 3. Profitability declined in August and September

Net Profit Margin fell to:

- **August 2025:** 14.39%
- **September 2025:** 14.43%

This period requires further investigation into COGS, pricing, and product mix.

### 4. Revenue was below budget

Actual revenue was below budget in **all 12 months** of the analysis period.

This highlights the need to review sales forecasting, regional performance, and product demand.

### 5. Operating expenses remained under control

Actual operating expenses were below budget in **all 12 months**, indicating effective cost discipline during the analysis period.

---

## 💡 CFO Recommendations

- Review pricing and COGS for low-margin products in Maharashtra.
- Reassess the product mix, particularly Electronics Accessories.
- Investigate the August–September profitability decline.
- Improve revenue forecasting using historical monthly and regional performance.
- Continue maintaining operating expense discipline.
- Focus on profitable revenue growth rather than revenue growth alone.
